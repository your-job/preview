Maintenance first, repairs when needed

The name says it: Southpaw does maintenance as well as handyman work. That makes it a good fit for homeowners who'd rather stay ahead of problems, and for landlords who need someone dependable on call for their units.

The business is based on West Oklahoma Avenue, close to Jackson Park and Southgate. Customers who've reviewed the work mention Shannon by name, for showing up on time and being easy to deal with from quote to finish.

Repairs and maintenance

Milwaukee's older homes, duplexes included, need steady attention. This is the work that keeps them comfortable for owners and tenants alike.
