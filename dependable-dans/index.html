What Dan can take care of

The four trades painted on the side of Dan's truck cover most of what goes wrong in a house. These are the jobs that come up most.

Plumbing fixes

Vanities, sinks, faucets and toilets swapped out, including cabinets that have rotted from a slow leak underneath.

Electrical

Light fixtures, switches and outlets replaced or added, so the dead outlet and the flickering ceiling light finally get sorted.

Carpentry and trim

Interior doors hung and cased, baseboards fitted, and the sticking door that never closed right adjusted until it does.

Painting

Rooms, trim and exterior siding scraped and repainted, from a single bedroom to a whole detached garage.

Kitchens and floors

Tile backsplashes, countertop updates and worn carpet replaced with plank flooring that's easy to keep clean.

Outside the house

Downspouts reattached, locks and handles replaced on storm doors, and the small exterior fixes that keep weather out.
